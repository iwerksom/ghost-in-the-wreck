Ghost in the Wreck · iteration 2 prototype

Play one run of the prototype, then fill this in. It takes about six minutes. Be blunt — this build exists to find out whether the ship-mind makes the game fun, not to be praised.

At the end of your run (or any time, from the PLAYTEST button in the top right of the game) you can copy a report code. Paste it in section 2 and the numbers fill themselves in.
